How it works

Every stage is designed to expose as little as possible.

  1. 01

    Unlock locally

    Your PIN stays in your browser. It derives a room hash and an in-memory encryption root.

  2. 02

    Prove access

    OPAQUE verifies that you know the PIN without sending the PIN to the server.

  3. 03

    Prepare a message

    The server issues a signed, one-time X25519 public key and a random message hash.

  4. 04

    Encrypt three times

    Your message is encrypted locally with three independent AES-256-GCM layers.

  5. 05

    Relay briefly

    A one-time transport layer delivers the ciphertext. The server keeps the encrypted payload in RAM for 15 seconds.

  6. 06

    Discover, not disclose

    Clients poll only temporary message hashes and timestamps. No message content appears in the list.

  7. 07

    Claim privately

    Your browser creates a fresh X25519 key pair and proves access with its OPAQUE session.

  8. 08

    Decrypt locally

    The browser verifies, decrypts the transport and three content layers, then discards one-time keys.

Your PIN, content keys, and message plaintext never leave your browser.
